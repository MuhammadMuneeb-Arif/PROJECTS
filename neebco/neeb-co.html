About the studio

Four people.
No account managers.
No filler slides.

NEEB & CO. is a small studio based in Lahore, working remotely with teams across three time zones. We keep every engagement to a team of two — one designer, one engineer — so nothing gets lost translating a Figma file into shipped code.

We take on six projects a year. That's a constraint we chose on purpose: it's the only way we've found to actually know the product we're designing for, instead of pattern-matching from the last client.
